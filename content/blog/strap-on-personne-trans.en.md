## The one criterion that really matters: how it holds {#hold}

Product pages describe material, length and diameter. None of those three tells you what happens ten minutes in.

**A harness-free hold** relies on an internal bulb, a soft silicone swell kept in place by contracting the pelvic floor. Nothing to strap, nothing to adjust, nothing showing under loose clothing. In exchange, the hold depends on a muscle that tires: excellent over a short session, less obvious over time.

**A strapped hold** inverts the trade-off exactly. A lingerie harness asks for no effort, stays put regardless, and is adjusted once and for all. But it shows, it is felt, and it adds a step to getting dressed.

**There is no universal right answer**, which is precisely why the question deserves to be asked before the first purchase rather than after. Most disappointments come from buying on diameter when the real subject was the hold.

## 1. Strap-on Classique New Version, the best harness-free entry point {#classic}

At **79.99 euros**, it is the simplest model in the harness-free family and the only one for which the brand states weights per size: **300 g in S, 335 g in M, 375 g in L and 400 g in XL**.

**That figure is the most useful thing on the whole page**, and almost nobody looks at it. A hundred grams between S and XL, on an object held by a muscle, completely changes how secure it feels. A model that is too heavy slips, and slipping ends the moment long before diameter does.

The silicone is soft, the finish seamless, the whole thing is waterproof and rinses clean. The point worth knowing: the triple stimulation the brand advertises depends on precise placement of the internal bulb, which takes two or three attempts to get right.

**The advice that avoids the mistake**: start at size S even if the diameter looks modest. The hold comes first, sizing up comes later.

## 2. Heroine Lingerie Harness, the best passive hold {#heroine}

At **49.99 euros**, in **XS to XXL**, it is the most affordable harness in the selection and the best rated on the brand's site. The logic is the reverse of the previous one: strap once, then stop thinking about it.

The size range matters more here than on a harness-free strap-on, because a badly fitted harness does not hold at all, whereas a badly sized internal strap-on merely holds poorly. Six sizes cover a noticeably wider spectrum than the market average.

It needs a dildo to go with it. The **P and G Spot Dildo**, at **39.99 euros**, comes in four sizes and remains the simplest choice for a first pairing.

## 3. The vibrating models, for a second purchase {#vibrating}

The **Vibrating Strap-on 3 Motors** at **129.99 euros** and the **Multi Orgasm Strap-on** at **149.99 euros** share the same harness-free hold as the classic model, with remote-controlled motors.

They are not a better first purchase, they are a different one. The hold is more demanding, since weight goes up, and the remote adds a variable to manage while the hold itself is not yet second nature. The Vibrating model also starts only at size M, which makes it less accessible for a first attempt.

## What a strap-on does not replace {#packer}

One point deserves stating plainly, because it causes most of the disappointed purchases on this ground.

**A strap-on is not a packer.** It is designed for occasional use, not for extended daily wear: holding it with the pelvic floor tires, silicone warms up, and the weight makes itself known after a few hours. A dedicated packer, lighter and with no penetrative function, answers a different need and does not compare on price.

The two objects overlap in search results, which explains the confusion. Telling them apart before buying avoids blaming a product for not doing what it was never built to do.

## Care, in three points {#care}

- **Wash with warm water and mild soap** after each use, or with a dedicated cleaner such as the Mixgliss at 10.90 euros. Waterproof silicone takes a rinse without trouble.
- **Water-based lubricant only.** A silicone lubricant attacks the surface of a silicone toy and makes it porous. Mixgliss Max at 10.90 euros for 70 ml does the job.
- **Air dry and store away from dust**, which soft silicone attracts strongly.
