## What it is, and what it is for {#definition}

An imitation semen lubricant is an ordinary intimate gel whose formula has been worked on three points: **colour**, a more or less opaque milky white, **density**, thicker than a clear lubricant, and **cohesion**, that tendency to form short strings rather than run like water.

## The criterion that actually matters: water-based or hybrid {#base}

This is the point almost no product page explains, and it is the one that can cost you a toy.

A **water-based** lubricant is compatible with everything: silicone, glass, steel and ABS toys, and latex condoms. It rinses off with plain water.

A **hybrid** lubricant contains a proportion of silicone, added to extend the glide. That share of silicone is enough to attack the surface of a silicone toy. The toy turns tacky, then porous, and a porous toy traps bacteria in micro-cavities that no washing reaches. The damage is cumulative and **irreversible**.

In this particular category the word hybrid is frequent, because a stringy texture is easier to achieve with a share of silicone. **The short rule: if you own a silicone toy, and that covers the vast majority of dildos and strap-ons sold today, stay water-based.**

## Texture hold, the criterion nobody tests {#texture}

This is the most frequently reported disappointment, and it makes sense. If you buy a lubricant for its appearance, a product that turns clear after thirty seconds simply does not do its job.

Two families coexist. Genuinely thickened formulas keep their opacity and their cohesion for several minutes, they can be spread without thinning out straight away. Cheap versions, often sold as novelty items, get their look from a colourant and a light thickener that do not survive contact and body heat.

No packaging shot lets you tell them apart. The only useful clues before buying are the stated density, the presence of precise texture vocabulary on the page, and reviews that explicitly mention hold.

## Precautions, short but real {#precautions}

- **Check the base before any other criterion.** It is the only point that can permanently ruin something you paid for.
- **On fabric, an opaque gel shows more than a clear lubricant.** A towel is enough.
- **None of these products has any effect on fertility, in either direction.** They are cosmetic lubricants, not medical devices, and they should not be presented otherwise.
- **If irritation occurs, stop.** As with any lubricant, individual sensitivity to a preservative or a fragrance is possible and entirely unremarkable.
