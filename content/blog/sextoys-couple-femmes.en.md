## The real criterion: who wears it, and does anyone wear it {#dynamic}

This is the question comparisons skip, and it determines everything else.

**A worn object assigns a role.** One partner wears, the other is penetrated. That is neither good nor bad, it is a dynamic, and plenty of couples are looking for it explicitly. The strap-on and the harness both belong to this family.

**A double dildo assigns nothing.** Both partners are penetrated at once, holding it is shared, and no active role is handed out in advance. It is a different experience, not a lesser version of the previous one.

**The third case, often forgotten, is taking turns.** A harness-free strap-on comes off and is handed over in seconds, which a strapped harness does not allow as easily. If the idea is to swap roles within the same evening, the harness-free model wins for a purely practical reason.

## Lubricant is not an accessory {#lubricant}

This is the point where no trade-off exists, and it is poorly explained almost everywhere.

**A silicone toy needs a water-based lubricant.** A silicone-based lubricant, or a hybrid formula containing silicone, attacks the surface of the toy. It turns tacky, then porous, and the damage is permanent: porous silicone holds residue and can no longer be properly sanitised.

In practice, a **[water-based lubricant](https://strap-on-me.com/en/collections/intimate-gel-anal-vaginal)** costs €9.90 to €14.90 and protects an object worth €79.99. The maths does itself.

The rule applies to storage too: two silicone toys should not be stored touching each other.

## What we left out, and why {#excluded}

**"Couples kits" under €30.** The silicone is rarely medical grade and the dimensions are rarely documented. One good object beats five mediocre ones, on this ground more than most.

**Rankings by audience.** An object is not designed for an orientation, it is designed for a use. Brands that segment their catalogue by audience sell the same thing under two labels. The useful criteria remain hold and dynamic.

**Solo vibrators.** They show up in most "for lesbian couples" lists while answering a different question. They have their place; it is not this one.
